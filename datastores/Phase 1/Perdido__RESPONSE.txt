Description:
The Operator charged the Perdido Joint Account $52,274.74 for CATS Payroll costs which appear to be administrative in nature
and covered by OH (SAP support specialists, data reporting techs, etc.). This was the subject of Perdido Information Request 13
issued to the Operator during the fieldwork.

The identified labor does not qualify for direct chargeability under the Billing Standards settlement agreements between the
parties, as they have not been adequately and properly documented by the Operator as activities required to comply with
regulatory activities, and they have not been identified as Planners & Schedulers, maintenance and turnaround, rig
administration whose job function was previously performed offshore/onsite but were relocated onshore. In addition, the
identified personnel did not have a job title that enabled the auditor to conclude the personnel possessed special & specific
engineering, geological, geophysical or operational skills absent proper documentation being provided by the Operator to
support this labor classification.

The Operator is requested to credit the Perdido Joint Account $52,274.74 with a reference to Chevron's audit number and
exception number on the Joint Interest Billing.


Operator's Reply on 27 March 2024

As per the Settlement Agreements, signed in 2021 and 2022 between Shell and Chevron / BP, and the Exhibit for Agreed OTL
Definition and Billing Standards, sections 3.A Regulatory Activity and 3.C Planners & Schedulers, maintenance and turnaround,
rig administration, show that both of these individuals are direct chargeable, as the nature of their jobs is technical.

Robert Indelicato's job profile from the staff listing shows he is a CMMS Engineer for Production Operations. As noted in the
attached spreadsheet he is within the Maintenance, Reliability and Turnaround job group. His work either qualified for section
3.A of the Billing Standards as regulatory work (as noted by the CFR codes) or section 3.C for maintenance and turnaround work
related to SAP support during these activities.

Tracey Champagne's job profile from the staff listing shows she is a CMMS Support for Maintenance. As noted in the attached
spreadsheet she is within the Maintenance, Reliability and Turnaround job group. Her work either qualified for section 3.A of
the Billing Standards as regulatory work (as noted by the CFR codes) or section 3.C for maintenance and turnaround work
related to SAP support during these activities.

No credit is due.

Description:
The Operator charged the Perdido Joint Account $157,928.70 for CATS Payroll costs which appear to be coding errors. Upon
review of Heli Logs and POBs, the employees were working at other locations.

This was the subject of Perdido Information Request 12, which the Operator responded, "The majority of the charges in this IR,
show the Perdido CATS time-writing was at a different period of time as to when the employees traveled to other assets. Their
time-writing was for regulatory work as shown by the CFR codes noted in column K or planners and schedulers. As per the
Settlement Agreements, signed in 2021 and 2022 between Shell and Chevron / BP, and their applicable Exhibit for Agreed OTL
Definition and Billing Standards, sections 3.A and 3.C show that both of these categories are direct chargeable. Thanks."

Upon review of the response spreadsheet provided by the Operator, there was no substantiation that the particular time
charged was for the Joint Account and not for other properties as noted in the workpapers provided. According to the
agreements regulatory work will be charged to the joint account provided such charges are pursuant to an approved AFE which
the regulatory activities must be adequately and properly documented by the Operator, identifying the specific activity of the
individuals and will reference specific program elements as described in the Federal Register. None of the AFE's being charged
in this exception reflect this regulatory designation prescribed by the settlement agreement. In addition, no additional support
was provided to reflect that the work being performed by the personnel identified as Planners & Schedulers as part of this
exception was performing a function that was previously performed offshore.

The Operator is requested to credit the Perdido Joint Account $157,928.70 with a reference to Chevron's audit number and
exception number on the Joint Interest Billing.


Operator's Reply on 27 March 2024
As was noted in the Audit Report regarding the employees working at other locations, the support doesn't seem to be attached
to verify this comment for the time period it was charged to the Joint Account. However, according to the Settlement
Agreement Billing Standards, and Exhibit C II of the Unit Operating Agreement the attached personnel are direct billable. The
Operators response remains the same as was provided during the audit field work.

No credit is due.
